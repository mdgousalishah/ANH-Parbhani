Official notice and terms

Please read these terms carefully before you apply.

  • All properties are leased for 11 months, 3 years or 30 years.
  • Leases are for business, residential or educational use only, as stated in your application.
  • A deposit is required to take part in the auction or bidding for new leases.
  • All tenants must follow the terms and conditions of the Maharashtra State Board of Waqfs.
