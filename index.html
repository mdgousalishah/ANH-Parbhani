A Living History, A Thriving Community

Established during the Hyderabad Nizam era.

For generations, the Ashoorkhana Nale Hyder & Masjid Mehtab Ali Shah complex has been a cornerstone of faith in Parbhani. Registered as a historic Waqf property under the Maharashtra State Board of Waqfs, our mission is to preserve these profound spiritual traditions and architectural marvels.

  • Authentic Nizam-era Architecture
  • Unbroken Chain of Stewardship (Silsila)
  • Dedicated to Community Welfare

Explore Our Sacred Complex

Discover the historic landmarks that make our premises unique.

Ashoorkhana

A sacred space for mourning and remembrance, central to spiritual life.

Naqqar Khana

The historic drum house, echoing traditions of announcing prayers.

Baradari

The twelve-doored pavilion, a timeless architectural marvel for gatherings.

Graveyard

A peaceful resting place, connecting us to the generations that built our community.

Masjid Services

Serving the spiritual and social needs of the Ummah.

Daily Salah

Congregational prayers held 5 times a day with facilities for wudu.

Jumu'ah Prayer

Weekly Friday sermons focusing on contemporary issues and Islamic guidance.

Quran Education

Madrasa classes for children and Tafseer sessions for adults.

Community Charity

Zakat distribution, food drives, and support for the underprivileged.

Nikah Services

Facilitating Islamic marriages within the premises with proper documentation.

Islamic Guidance

Counseling and fatwa services for personal and family matters.
