Guardians of Faith & Heritage

The Ashoorkhana Naale Hyder & Masjid Mehtab Ali Shah is a living institution rooted in the Nizam era.

Our Silsila (Chain of Succession) documents an unbroken lineage of caretakers dedicated to the service of Allah and the welfare of the community.

Masjid Etiquette (Adab)

To maintain the sanctity of the House of Allah, please observe the following:

  • Dress modestly and cleanly.
  • Enter with your right foot first.
  • Keep mobile phones on silent.
  • Avoid worldly talk inside the prayer hall.
