A living history, a thriving community

Established during the Hyderabad Nizam era.

For generations, the Ashoorkhana Naale Hyder and Masjid e Mehtab Ali Shah complex has been a cornerstone of faith in Parbhani. Registered as a historic Waqf property with the Maharashtra State Board of Waqfs, our mission is to preserve its spiritual traditions and its architecture.

  • Authentic Nizam-era architecture
  • An unbroken chain of stewardship (Silsila)
  • Dedicated to community welfare

Ashoorkhana

A sacred space for mourning and remembrance, central to the spiritual life of the complex.

Naqqar Khana

The historic drum house, echoing the old tradition of announcing prayers and occasions.

Baradari

The twelve-doored pavilion, a timeless piece of architecture for gatherings.

Graveyard

A peaceful resting place that connects us to the generations who built this community.

Masjid services

Serving the spiritual and social needs of the Ummah.

  • Daily salah

    Congregational prayers five times a day, with facilities for wudu.

  • Jumu'ah prayer

    Weekly Friday sermons on contemporary issues and Islamic guidance.

  • Quran education

    Madrasa classes for children and Tafseer sessions for adults.

  • Community charity

    Zakat distribution, food drives and support for the underprivileged.

  • Nikah services

    Islamic marriages within the premises, with proper documentation.

  • Islamic guidance

    Counselling and fatwa services for personal and family matters.
