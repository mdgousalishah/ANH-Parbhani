A Cornerstone of Faith in Parbhani

The Ashoorkhana Nale Hyder & Masjid Mehtab Ali Shah complex is a deeply revered Nizam-era sanctuary. Officially registered as a historic Waqf property (MSBW/PBN/47/2010), our mission goes beyond preservation. We strive to maintain the profound spiritual traditions of our ancestors while actively serving the educational, social, and spiritual needs of our modern community.

Our Official Journey

Tracing our legacy through key foundational milestones.

1869–1911

The Nizam's Grant (Inaam)

H.H. Sir Mir Mahboob Ali Khan

The foundation begins with a historic "Inaam" (grant) of land from the 6th Nizam of Hyderabad. This generous grant established the land as a sacred endowment for generations.

Shawal 1302 Hijri (c. 1885)

Original Endowment Deed

The historic 'Sanad' (deed) bearing the seal of the Nizam's government was signed, officially recording the grant of land and cementing its Waqf status.

September 26, 1961

Official Trust Registration

The trust was officially registered under the Bombay Public Trust Act, securing its legal status as Public Trust No. B.IS 123/61.

January 27, 1987

Administrative Scheme

The official scheme for the administration and management of the trust was formally adopted by legal authorities, outlining its governance structure.

Present Day

Preserving the Legacy

Today, our dedicated leadership continues the mission, focusing on community service, spiritual guidance, and protecting the historic Waqf land against modern challenges.

Architectural Marvels

Discover the Qutub Shahi landmarks that define our sacred complex.

Ashoorkhana Nal Saheb

The spiritual heart of our complex, dedicated to the remembrance of Imam Hussain (AS) and hosting sacred gatherings.

Naqqar Khana

The historic drum house, a classic feature of Nizam-era architecture used to announce prayer times and events.

Baradari

The timeless twelve-doored pavilion, designed for ventilation and elegance, serving as a space for community gatherings.

Graveyard

The complex includes a historic graveyard and a Musafirkhana (guest house), both vital parts of the original endowment.

Community Services

Serving our community's spiritual, educational, and modern needs.

Computer Institute

We host a 24-hour computer institute, providing valuable technical education to students from all backgrounds.

Ujra-e-Nal Saheb

An establishment providing cooling sharbat and 'Shital Jal' (cold water) to devotees, continuing a tradition of hospitality.

Modern Security

To ensure the safety and security of all visitors and devotees, the entire complex is monitored by 24/7 CCTV cameras.

Religious Education

Offering comprehensive Madrasa classes, Arabic language courses, and Islamic history sessions for the youth.

Community Events

Hosting a variety of community events, from vibrant Eid celebrations to educational lectures and health camps.

Charity Support

Dedicated to uplifting the marginalized through organized Zakat distribution, food drives, and emergency relief.
