A cornerstone of faith in Parbhani

The Ashoorkhana Naale Hyder and Masjid e Mehtab Ali Shah complex is a deeply revered Nizam-era sanctuary. Officially registered as a historic Waqf property (MSBW/PBN/47/2010), our mission goes beyond preservation: we keep the spiritual traditions of our ancestors alive while serving the educational, social and spiritual needs of our community today.

Our official journey

Tracing our legacy through its founding milestones.

  1. 1869–1911

    The Nizam's grant (Inaam)

    H.H. Sir Mir Mahboob Ali Khan

    The foundation begins with a historic "Inaam" (grant) of land from the 6th Nizam of Hyderabad. This generous grant established the land as a sacred endowment for generations.

    H.H. Sir Mir Mahboob Ali Khan,
    6th Nizam of Hyderabad
  2. Shawal 1302 Hijri
    (c. 1885)

    Original endowment deed

    The historic Sanad (deed) bearing the seal of the Nizam's government was signed, recording the grant of land and cementing its Waqf status.

  3. 26 September 1961

    Official trust registration

    The trust was registered under the Bombay Public Trust Act, securing its legal status as Public Trust No. B.IS 123/61.

  4. 27 January 1987

    Administrative scheme

    The official scheme for the administration and management of the trust was formally adopted by the legal authorities, setting out its governance.

  5. Present day

    Preserving the legacy

    Today our leadership continues the mission, focusing on community service, spiritual guidance and protecting the historic Waqf land against modern challenges.

Guided by a commitment to preserve and serve.

Architectural heritage

The Qutub Shahi landmarks that define our sacred complex.

Ashoorkhana Nal Saheb

The spiritual heart of the complex, dedicated to the remembrance of Imam Hussain (AS) and home to sacred gatherings.

Naqqar Khana

The historic drum house, a classic feature of Nizam-era architecture used to announce prayer times and events.

Baradari

The timeless twelve-doored pavilion, built for airflow and elegance and used for community gatherings.

Graveyard and Musafirkhana

A historic graveyard and a guest house for travellers, both part of the original endowment.

Community services

Serving our community's spiritual, educational and everyday needs.

  • Computer institute

    A 24-hour computer institute providing technical education to students from all backgrounds.

  • Ujra-e-Nal Saheb

    Cooling sharbat and Shital Jal (cold water) for devotees, continuing a tradition of hospitality.

  • Safety and security

    The whole complex is monitored by CCTV cameras around the clock for visitors and devotees.

  • Religious education

    Madrasa classes, Arabic language courses and Islamic history sessions for the youth.

  • Community events

    From Eid celebrations to educational lectures and health camps.

  • Charity support

    Organised Zakat distribution, food drives and emergency relief for those in need.
