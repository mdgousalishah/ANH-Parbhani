Our Story

A Beacon of Faith and Heritage Since the Nizam Era

Our Journey Through Time

Tracing a legacy of faith from its historic origins to the present day.

October 1880 (Shawal 1302 Hijri)

Foundation of the Endowment

The complex is established as a service endowment (Khidmat-e-Mash) through the historic deed Muntaqab No. 25624, with Mahemood Ali Shah Roshan Ali Shah as the first Mutawalli.

May 2, 1974

Official Waqf Recognition

The property is officially recognized and registered as a Waqf in the Maharashtra Government Gazette, cementing its legal status as a protected charitable endowment.

1959

Land Lease Agreement

A portion of the endowed land (4 acres from Survey No. 2) is leased to the Parbhani Municipal Council for public use, highlighting the Masjid's early role in civic life.

September 25, 2025

Leadership Reaffirmed

An official corrigendum reaffirms the details of the Waqf and confirms the appointment of Sajjada Nashin Va Mutawally Mohammad Mushtaque Ahmed as the current caretaker.

Present Day

Preserving the Legacy

The current leadership continues the mission, focusing on community service, spiritual guidance, and actively working to protect the historic Waqf land from illegal encroachment for future generations.

Architectural Marvels

Discover the historic landmarks that define our sacred complex.

Ashoorkhana Nale Hyder

The spiritual heart of our complex. A sacred space dedicated to the remembrance of Imam Hussain (AS), hosting gatherings for mourning and reflection, especially during Muharram.

Naqqar Khana

The historic drum house, a classic feature of Nizam-era architecture. It was traditionally used to announce prayer times and important events, its echoes a link to our vibrant past.

Baradari

The twelve-doored pavilion, designed for ventilation and elegance. This timeless structure serves as a beautiful open space for community gatherings, meetings, and serene contemplation.

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to serve as a vibrant center for Islamic life, dedicated to spiritual growth, education, and community service. We strive to preserve our rich heritage while fostering an inclusive environment where all can gather, learn, and worship in peace and harmony.
