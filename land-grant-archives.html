Historical Archives

Digitized records of the 1885 Nizam-era Sanad and land grant documents establishing the Waqf.

Original Document

The Royal Grant (1885)

This Sanad (Muntakhab) confirms the endowment of 13 acres 23 gunthas of land by H.H. Nizam VI to the first Mutawalli, Haji Syed Mahemood Ali Shah. It serves as the primary legal proof of the Waqf property.

Official Gazette Record

Government Gazette notification listing the property as a registered Waqf under the Waqf Act. This document validates the continuity of the institution's legal status post-independence.
