Historic Waqf Land Grants

Original, high-resolution documentation from the Nizam era confirming the endowment and legal status of the Masjid e Mehtab Ali Shah complex.

Legal Importance

These historical records confirm the original Waqf Endowment of the Masjid land. They serve as essential, irrefutable evidence for protecting the property from illegal encroachment and ensuring the preservation of the institution's heritage under the Waqf Act.

This historic document, dating back to the Nizam Era, is the absolute cornerstone of our institution's history and legal standing. It meticulously details the original service endowment, known formally as Khidmat-e-Mash, of over 13+ Acres of land granted exclusively for the establishment and continuous maintenance of the Masjid e Mehtab Ali Shah complex and the Ashoorkhana Nale Hyder.

The grant was established as a sacred trust, intended to ensure that the complex would serve as an eternal center for prayer, community gathering, and spiritual remembrance for generations to come. The document strictly outlines the specific survey numbers and boundaries of the land, underscoring its religious significance and inalienable status as a Waqf property.

"This endowment was not merely a gift of land, but a divine commitment to the future of our community—a promise that this sacred space would be protected, maintained, and preserved until the end of times."

Today, as the institution faces modern challenges, this document is more important than ever. It is the primary reference in our ongoing legal and administrative efforts to address illegal encroachments. Preserving this land is not just about protecting property; it is a religious obligation to honor the intentions of our founders and safeguard a vital piece of our spiritual heritage.

Chronology of Endowment

1880
Original Endowment Issued

The 'Sanad' is granted by the Nizam Government, officially establishing the Khidmat-e-Mash.

1961
Trust Registration

Registered under the Bombay Public Trust Act, securing its legal status in modern India.

1987
Administrative Scheme

An official legal scheme for the administration and management of the trust is adopted.

Present Day
Active Protection

Current Mutawalli and Waqf Board actively use these documents to reclaim encroached land.
