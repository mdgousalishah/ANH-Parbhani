Common questions

Answers to the questions we hear most.

You can donate by bank transfer to our Canara Bank account or scan the UPI QR code in the donation section above. Funds are used for masjid maintenance, educational programs and helping those in need in the community.

We encourage community participation. Use the WhatsApp form above, choose "Volunteering" as the topic, and tell us your skills. We often need help with events, tutoring and maintenance.

We offer a weekend Islamic Studies Program for youth aged 7 to 14, adult Tafseer sessions, and a 24-hour computer institute open to students of all backgrounds. See the youth program.

Download the rent agreement PDF from the leasing section to review the terms. When you're ready, fill in the registration form, contact the Mutawalli's office, or send an inquiry with the WhatsApp form.
