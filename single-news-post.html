New Mutawalli appointed by the Maharashtra State Waqf Board

1 August 2025 Official announcement 3 min read
Admin officeMasjid e Mehtab Ali Shah Trust, Parbhani, Maharashtra

We are honoured to announce the official appointment of Sajjada Nashin va Mutawally Mohammad Mushtaque Ahmed Mohammad Ali Shah as the new Mutawalli of the Masjid e Mehtab Ali Shah complex. The appointment was formally approved by the Maharashtra State Waqf Board, continuing a legacy of hereditary stewardship rooted in the institution's founding.

A Mutawalli is the official caretaker or trustee of a Waqf (an Islamic endowment of property), responsible for its administration. This sacred role ensures that the institution's assets, originally endowed in October 1880 under the historic deed Muntaqab No. 25624, are protected and used for their intended charitable, educational and religious purposes.

"I am humbled by this responsibility and deeply dedicated to upholding the values, protecting the land, and continuing the traditions of this sacred institution for future generations. Our work has only just begun."

Mohammed Mushtaque Ahmed

Mr. Ahmed brings a deep commitment to preserving this rich Qutub Shahi heritage. Over the past months he has pledged to work closely with the community and government officials. His primary focus is the long-standing issue of illegal encroachment on the Masjid's endowed 13 acres and 23 gunthas of land.

The community has expressed strong support for his appointment and is optimistic about the future of the complex under his leadership. His vision includes not only protecting the Waqf property legally but also expanding the community services we offer, including youth education and charity. We look forward to a new chapter of growth, preservation and unity.
