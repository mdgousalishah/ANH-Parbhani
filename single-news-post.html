New Mutawalli Appointed for the Trust

Aug 01, 2025 Waqf Board
The Maharashtra State Board of Waqfs has officially announced the appointment of Janab Mohammed Mushtaque Ahmed as the new Mutawalli (Administrator) of the Masjid e Mehtab Ali Shah and Ashoorkhana Naale Hyder trust.

In a ceremony held at the district Waqf office, the official order was handed over, marking a new chapter in the administration of this historic 19th-century institution.

Vision for the Future

Addressing the community, Janab Mushtaque Ahmed outlined his priorities:

  • Legal Protection: Continuing the fight to reclaim encroached Waqf land.
  • Education: Expanding the Maktab program for children.
  • Transparency: Digitizing all financial records and donation tracking.

"I am a servant of this house of Allah. My only goal is to restore its glory and ensure it serves the poor and needy as intended by our ancestors."

The community has welcomed this decision, hoping for a swift resolution to pending development projects.
