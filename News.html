Legal victory

Minority Commission orders municipality to return land

26 Dec 2024

In a major victory for the Waqf, the Maharashtra State Minority Commission has ordered the Parbhani Municipal Commissioner to hand over possession of the leased Waqf land back to the institution.

The commission's decision follows a complaint filed by the Mutawalli about the land whose lease expired in 1989. It is a significant step towards reclaiming the property and using it for the educational and spiritual upliftment of the community, as originally intended by the Nizam's grant.

Legal action

610 individuals receive notices

14 Jun 2025

In a second phase of decisive action, the Maharashtra State Waqf Board has issued legal notices to 610 occupants on the Masjid's land.

The notice warns of criminal proceedings under the Waqf Act if they do not give a satisfactory explanation for their occupation. The move has been widely welcomed by the community.
