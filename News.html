Legal Victory

Minority Commission Orders Municipality to Return Land

Dec 26, 2024

In a major victory for the Waqf, the Maharashtra State Minority Commission has ordered the Parbhani Municipal Commissioner to hand over the possession of the leased Waqf land back to the institution.

The commission's decision comes after a complaint filed by the Mutawalli regarding the land for which the lease expired in 1989. This is a significant step towards reclaiming the property and utilizing it for the educational and spiritual upliftment of the community as originally intended by the Nizam's grant.

Legal Action

610 Individuals Receive Notices

Jun 14, 2025

In a second phase of decisive action, the Maharashtra State Waqf Board has issued legal notices to 610 occupants on the Masjid's land.

The notice explicitly warns of criminal proceedings under the strict guidelines of the Waqf Act if they do not provide a satisfactory explanation for their occupation. This bold move has been widely welcomed by the community.
