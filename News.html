Legal Update
BREAKING NEWS

Minority Commission Orders Return of Land

The Maharashtra State Minority Commission has issued a landmark order directing the Parbhani Municipal Corporation to return the leased Waqf land.

In a major victory for the Waqf, the commission's decision comes after a detailed hearing of the complaint filed by the Mutawalli. The 30-year lease expired in 1989, and the land has been held by the corporation since. This order mandates immediate action to hand over possession back to the institution.

Encroachment Update

610 Notices Issued to Occupants

The Waqf Board has intensified its drive against illegal encroachment. Notices under the Waqf Act have been served to 610 individuals and shopkeepers occupying the Masjid's land. They have been given 4 weeks to prove their legal standing or face eviction proceedings.

Community Announcement

New Mutawalli Appointed

The Maharashtra State Board of Waqfs has officially appointed Mohammed Mushtaque Ahmed as the Mutawalli of the institution. He pledges to continue the legal battle for the land and improve community services.
