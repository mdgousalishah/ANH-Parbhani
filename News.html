Legal Update

Minority Commission Orders Return of Land

A historic victory for the Waqf institution.

The State Commission has directed the Municipal Corporation to hand over possession of the 4 acres 36 gunthas land back to the Masjid trust immediately, citing the expiry of the lease in 1989. This follows years of legal struggle and representations made by the Managing Committee.

Encroachment Action

610 Notices Issued to Occupants

The Waqf Board has intensified its drive against illegal encroachment on the Masjid's land. Official notices under the Waqf Act have been served to 610 individuals and shopkeepers.

Occupants have been given a strict timeline to prove their legal standing or face eviction proceedings. This move is part of the larger effort to reclaim Waqf properties across the state.

Leadership Announcement

New Mutawalli Appointed

The Maharashtra State Board of Waqfs has officially appointed Janab Mohammed Mushtaque Ahmed as the new Mutawalli of the institution.

In his inaugural address, he pledged to prioritize transparency, educational welfare for the community, and the conservation of the heritage structures within the complex.
