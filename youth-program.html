Enroll Your Child

What We Learn

A comprehensive curriculum designed for young minds.

Quran & Tajweed

Learning correct pronunciation, recitation rules, and memorization of short Surahs.

Seerah & History

Inspiring stories from the life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) and other Prophets.

Adab & Akhlaq

Developing Islamic manners, respect for parents, kindness, and good character.

Fiqh & Salah

Practical guidance on Wudu, Salah performance, and daily Duas.

How We Teach

We believe education should be engaging, not just memorization.

Interactive Discussions

Students are encouraged to ask "Why?" and "How?" to build a deeper understanding of their faith.

Storytelling

We use stories from the Quran and Seerah to teach moral lessons in a way that resonates with children.

Mentorship

Our teachers act as mentors, guiding students in their personal and spiritual development.

Parent's FAQ

We charge a nominal monthly fee to cover the cost of materials and facility maintenance. However, scholarships are available for families in need. No child will be turned away due to lack of funds.

Currently, the program is open to boys and girls between the ages of 7 and 14. We divide them into two groups (7-10 and 11-14) for age-appropriate learning.

We provide all necessary textbooks and notebooks. Students should bring a pencil case and a water bottle. We recommend modest dress code suitable for the Masjid environment.
