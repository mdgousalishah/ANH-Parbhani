Program Overview

We are excited to offer this comprehensive program designed to build a strong foundation in Islamic knowledge and values. Our curriculum is tailored to be both educational and inspiring for young minds.

Classes are held in a supportive and welcoming environment where students are encouraged to ask questions, engage in discussions, and connect with their faith on a deeper level.

Enroll Your Child
Curriculum

Quranic studies, Seerah of the Prophets, Islamic history, and essential daily prayers (Duas).

Age Group

Specifically designed for youth ages 7 through 14. Boys and girls are welcome.

Schedule

Saturday & Sunday mornings.
10:00 AM to 1:00 PM.

Location

Why Choose This Program?

Benefits of enrolling your child in our weekend studies.

Strong Foundation

Build a solid base of Islamic knowledge (Aqeedah) and values that will guide them for a lifetime.

Engaging Lessons

Interactive, age-appropriate lessons utilizing modern teaching techniques to make learning enjoyable.

Community Bonding

A chance for children to connect with peers in a positive, faith-based environment, fostering lifelong friendships.

Character Development

Emphasis on Akhlaq (manners) and Adab (etiquette) to shape them into responsible, caring individuals.
