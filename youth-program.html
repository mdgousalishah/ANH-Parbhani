Program overview

A comprehensive program that builds a strong foundation in Islamic knowledge and values, with a curriculum that is both educational and inspiring for young minds.

Classes are held in a supportive, welcoming environment where students are encouraged to ask questions, join discussions and connect with their faith more deeply.

Enrol your child

  • Curriculum

    Quranic studies, Seerah of the Prophets, Islamic history and essential daily prayers (Duas).

  • Age group

    Designed for children aged 7 to 14. Boys and girls are welcome.

  • Schedule

    Saturday and Sunday mornings, 10:00 am to 1:00 pm.

  • Location

    Community Education Hall, Masjid e Mehtab Ali Shah complex.

Why choose this program?

What your child gains from our weekend studies.

Strong foundation

A solid base of Islamic knowledge (Aqeedah) and values to guide them for a lifetime.

Engaging lessons

Interactive, age-appropriate lessons that make learning enjoyable.

Community bonding

Children connect with peers in a positive, faith-based environment and make lasting friendships.

Character development

Emphasis on Akhlaq (manners) and Adab (etiquette) to raise responsible, caring individuals.
